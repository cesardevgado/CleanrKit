{% elif page_key == 'about' %}

Useful cleanup without the friction

CleanrKit is a growing collection of focused tools for cleaning, formatting, and validating text and structured data. It was created around a simple idea: everyday cleanup work should be fast, understandable, and available without installing software or creating an account.

Privacy-first by design

The cleaners are designed to process your input in the browser whenever possible. CleanrKit does not require an account, and the text or data you enter into a cleaner is not intentionally retained as user content.

Independent and human

Built by Cesar Delgado

CleanrKit is an independent product created and maintained by Cesar Delgado, a designer and developer focused on building practical, approachable digital tools.

Still in beta

{% elif page_key == 'privacy' %}

Overview

CleanrKit is built to minimize the personal information needed to use its tools. You can use the cleaners without registering or creating a profile.

Content you process

CleanrKit does not intentionally store the text, code, or structured data you enter into a cleaner as user content. Processing may involve requests to the application server to produce a result, but input is not designed to be saved to a user account or content database. Avoid submitting secrets or highly sensitive information to any online service.

Technical and usage data

Like most websites, hosting infrastructure may process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps for security, reliability, and diagnostics.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

CleanrKit may use local browser storage to remember preferences such as theme. The site may also use third-party analytics or advertising services, including Google AdSense, which may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies.

Third-party links

Links to external websites are governed by those websites’ policies. CleanrKit is not responsible for their privacy practices.

Your choices and contact

You can restrict cookies through your browser and leave fields empty when contacting us if information is optional. For privacy questions, use the contact page.
